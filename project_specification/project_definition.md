# Project Definition

## Name
Market Intelligence Data Platform

## Strategic Purpose
An end-to-end analytics platform designed to ingest, validate, transform, and analyze tech-job market data. It prioritizes remote job opportunities where Spanish and current geographic contexts (Lima/Argentina) provide optimal access conditions, while keeping international markets in scope for market demand analysis.

## Core Objective
Answer three strategic business questions:
1. What roles, technologies, and skills are currently demanded across targeted markets?
2. How does global market demand differ from current candidate accessibility (geography & language)?
3. What are the priority learning and skill gaps needed to increase market penetration?
